## 3. App Review Notes (Apple İnceleme Ekibi Test Adımları)
1. **No Account Required:** The app requires no sign-up or login to test every feature (Guideline 4.8).
2. **Test 1 — Offline Mode (Guideline 4.2 Native Functionality):**
   - Enable **Airplane Mode** OR tap the **Offline Lock** toggle in the top-right bar.
   - Tap **"Try Sample File"** (or choose any HEIC/PNG/JPG photo from Photos/Files).
   - Select **YAML**, **CSV**, **PNG**, **JPG**, or **PDF** (marked green `Local`), optionally toggle **Strip EXIF/GPS**, and tap **Start Conversion**.
   - Conversion completes instantaneously 100% on-device using native Swift `ImageIO` / `PDFKit` / `Vision` and local engines, with inline Preview and native Share Sheet.
3. **Test 2 — Transparent Warnings & Offline Lock Guard:**
   - With Offline Lock enabled, tap any purple `Cloud` target (e.g., `PARQUET` or `DOCX -> PNG`). The app blocks outbound network calls and presents a clear single-job confirmation dialog.
4. **Test 3 — In-App Purchases (Guideline 3.1.1):**
   - Open **Settings → Credit Packs & Pro** to inspect consumable never-expiring credit packs, monthly/yearly Pro subscriptions, clear auto-renewal disclosure, Privacy Policy / Terms links, and the working **Restore Purchases** button wired directly to StoreKit 2 (`AppStore.sync()`).
5. **Privacy & Export Compliance:**
   - Zero third-party analytics, zero advertising SDKs, zero IDFA/ATT tracking (`NSPrivacyTracking = false`), and `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false`.
